Economic Growth

CDA Registered Cooperative

We are proud to be a CDA Registered Cooperative, meeting the highest standards of verified economic performance and community accountability in the Philippines.

By partnering with local suppliers and showcasing Filipino brands within our network, we contribute to a vibrant local economy. From the coffee we serve to the furniture in our lounges, we prioritize locally sourced products that share our vision for quality and community impact.
